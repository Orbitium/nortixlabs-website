# Islandly Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-25

This Privacy Policy explains how Islandly, NortixLabs, and their operators ("Islandly," "we," "us," or "our") collect, use, store, disclose, and otherwise process information when you use the Islandly mobile application, related websites, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Your information will be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to information processed through the Islandly app and related account, sync, subscription, support, and profile-sharing features.

This Policy does not necessarily apply to third-party services that you may access through Islandly, including Apple, Google, Firebase, Sign in with Apple, or other third-party systems. Those providers may have their own privacy policies and terms.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information.

### Information you provide or generate in the app

This may include:

- your email address if you choose to register or sign in with email and password;
- your display name;
- your profile tag;
- your avatar, theme, and profile appearance choices;
- your profile visibility setting;
- support messages, bug reports, feedback, or other communications you intentionally send to us; and
- reports or blocks you submit about another user's profile or display name.

### Authentication and account information

Depending on how you sign in, this may include:

- Firebase Authentication identifiers;
- anonymous guest account identifiers;
- Google Sign-In account identifiers;
- Sign in with Apple account identifiers; and
- limited profile information provided by those sign-in services, such as name or email where made available by the provider and by your settings.

### Focus, progression, and island data

To provide the app experience, we may process:

- focus session status, planned minutes, start and end times, pause state, title, subtitle, and related reward data;
- progression data such as levels, XP, streak values, and related timestamps;
- island state, tiles, placeables, stored work values, and island upgrade state;
- resource balances such as work, dirt, wood, and crystals;
- streak shield usage records;
- public profile visit counts and related profile metrics; and
- sync timestamps, pending sync actions, and related metadata required to reconcile local and remote app state.

### Purchase and entitlement information

If you subscribe to Islandly Premium or restore a purchase, we may process:

- App Store or Google Play product identifiers;
- transaction or purchase verification data;
- entitlement status;
- expiration or subscription status information returned by the store or our verification systems; and
- backend records needed to activate or restore premium access.

### Device, app, and technical information

We may also process:

- device and app metadata needed to operate the Service;
- authentication tokens or session metadata;
- local settings stored on your device;
- notification permission state and scheduling metadata;
- crash, error, logging, diagnostics, or operational records related to account, sync, purchase, moderation, or app stability; and
- network request and response information necessary to secure and operate the Service.

## 3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

- provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Service;
- create, authenticate, recover, and manage anonymous or signed-in sessions;
- save and sync your profile, island, focus, progression, and other app data;
- process, verify, and restore subscriptions or other premium entitlements;
- support public profile discovery and profile sharing when you choose to enable those features;
- enforce our rules, review moderation signals, and protect users from abuse or misuse;
- allow users to report or block other users within the app;
- send reminders, focus completion notifications, and iOS Live Activities where supported and enabled;
- detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, security incidents, unauthorized access, or policy violations;
- provide support, respond to inquiries, and troubleshoot technical problems;
- comply with legal obligations, lawful requests, or enforcement needs; and
- establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction that requires a legal basis for processing, we generally rely on one or more of the following:

- contract necessity, where processing is needed to provide the Service you requested, such as account access, sync, purchases, and core app functionality;
- legitimate interests, where processing is reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, reliability, diagnostics, product improvement, moderation, and protection of the Service and its users;
- consent, where we ask for it, such as for certain notifications, permissions, or optional features where consent is required by law; and
- legal obligations, where processing is required to comply with applicable law, lawful requests, accounting, tax, or regulatory requirements.

## 5. Public Profiles, Discovery, Sharing, Reports, and Blocking

Islandly includes optional public profile features.

If you enable discoverability, your display name may appear to other users in Islandly's discovery surfaces. Shared public profile links may expose additional public profile presentation and island information that is meant to be shareable by design.

Islandly also includes in-app tools that allow users to:

- report inappropriate display names; and
- block other users.

When a user is blocked, we may use that block relationship to limit visibility between those users inside the Service.

You should not enable public profile features or share your profile link if you do not want your public profile information to be visible to others.

## 6. Purchases and Billing

Premium subscriptions and other in-app purchases are processed by Apple or Google through their respective stores.

We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

We may receive limited transaction, product, receipt, verification, or entitlement information needed to:

- validate that a purchase was completed;
- restore access to premium features;
- prevent fraud or misuse; and
- update your premium status across devices and backend systems.

Subscription pricing, billing, renewal, cancellation, refund handling, and store-specific rights are governed in part by the App Store or Google Play.

## 7. Device Permissions and Local Features

Depending on your device, platform, and settings, Islandly may request or use:

- notification permissions for reminders and focus-complete notifications;
- local on-device storage, including a local database and app preferences;
- network access to sync data, verify purchases, and support account features; and
- iOS Live Activities for active focus sessions on supported Apple devices.

You can usually manage device permissions through your device settings. Some features may not function fully if required permissions are denied.

## 8. How We Share Information

We may share information in the following circumstances:

- with service providers, vendors, hosting providers, infrastructure providers, and technical partners that help us operate the Service;
- with authentication providers such as Firebase Authentication, Google, or Apple when you use those sign-in methods;
- with Firebase and related Google services where used for authentication, backend infrastructure, and diagnostics;
- with Apple or Google when needed to process or restore subscriptions or purchases;
- with other users when you intentionally make your profile public or share a public profile link;
- when needed to investigate reports, enforce our rules, or protect the safety, rights, property, or security of users, us, or third parties;
- when required by law, regulation, legal process, court order, or valid governmental request; and
- in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, asset sale, bankruptcy, or similar business transaction.

At the time of this Policy, we do not use a separate third-party mobile product analytics SDK in the app itself. We may use service-provider diagnostics, operational logs, and Firebase-related services where needed to run, secure, and troubleshoot the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to:

- provide the Service;
- preserve sync integrity and account continuity;
- restore purchases and entitlements;
- respond to support or legal issues;
- detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or security incidents;
- maintain required business and legal records; and
- comply with legal, tax, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

Retention may vary based on the type of data, whether the account is anonymous or authenticated, whether the data is needed for abuse prevention or dispute resolution, and whether the law requires us to keep certain records for a period of time.

Examples of how retention may work include:

- account, sync, island, progression, and profile data are generally retained until deletion, prolonged inactivity, or loss of an anonymous account, subject to lawful retention needs;
- purchase, subscription, and entitlement records may be retained for as long as needed for restoration, fraud prevention, chargeback handling, and tax or accounting compliance;
- support messages, moderation reports, and block records may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to review issues, enforce rules, and protect users;
- crash logs, operational logs, and diagnostics may be retained for a limited period needed to investigate bugs, incidents, and stability issues; and
- some aggregated or de-identified records may be retained longer where they no longer reasonably identify you.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

- request access to personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request deletion of your information, subject to lawful exceptions;
- request a copy of certain data in a portable format where required by law;
- object to or request restriction of certain processing in some circumstances; and
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights may be subject to legal limitations, identity verification, and exceptions permitted by law.

## 11. Account Deletion

If you created or use an account in Islandly, you can initiate account deletion from within the app.

When an account deletion request is completed, we aim to delete the account and associated app data from our active systems except where we are legally permitted or required to retain certain data for legitimate reasons such as security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, taxation, accounting, legal compliance, or enforcement.

The informational account deletion page at `https://nortixlabs.com/islandly/account-deletion` may provide additional guidance, but the app itself supports in-app initiation of deletion.

Deleting the app from your device alone does not necessarily delete your backend account or server-side records.

## 12. Your Choices

Depending on your location, platform, and how you use the Service, you may be able to:

- use the app anonymously instead of linking a permanent sign-in;
- sign in with email, Google, or Apple where those methods are offered;
- choose whether your profile is public or private;
- decide whether to share your public profile link;
- report or block other users in supported areas of the app;
- restore purchases through the relevant app store;
- manage notifications through device settings; and
- request account deletion in the app.

## 13. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children who are not legally able to consent to data processing in their jurisdiction without required parental or guardian involvement.

If you believe a child provided us personal information in violation of applicable law, contact us so we can review the situation.

## 14. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of transmission over the internet, authentication system, or electronic storage system is completely secure. Because of that, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 15. International Processing

Your information may be processed, stored, or transferred in countries or regions other than the one where you live, depending on where we or our service providers operate.

For example, if we use providers such as Google, Firebase, Apple, or other cloud and infrastructure partners, some processing may occur in the United States or in other countries where those providers maintain systems, personnel, or subprocessors.

Where required, we will rely on appropriate measures for those transfers under applicable law.

## 16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make material changes, we may provide notice through the Service, on our website, or by other reasonable means. The "Effective date" at the top of this Policy indicates when the current version took effect.

## 17. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, or account-related requests, contact us at `privacy@nortixlabs.com`.

You may also visit `https://nortixlabs.com` for general support information.
